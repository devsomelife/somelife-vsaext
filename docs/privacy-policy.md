# Privacy Policy

VSA Ext is a Chrome extension that helps you fill in your own VSA timesheet. It does not collect, send or sell any data.

## Data Read

- The client and project names shown in the dropdowns of your own VSA timesheet page, so you can pick them in the extension.
- The language of that page.

The extension only runs on the VSA address you enter yourself.

## Data Stored

Stored only in your browser, on this computer (`chrome.storage.local`):

- The time entries and notes you type.
- The client and project list read from VSA.
- Your settings: VSA address, language, CRA tab name and CRA workbook address.

## Data Shared

- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- When filling in your timesheet, the extension uses your VSA page the way you would by hand, so requests go only to your own VSA site.
- Data leaves the extension only when you choose to: `Copy for CRA sheet` puts the month on your clipboard, and `Export JSON` or `Export CSV` saves a file.

## Permissions

- `storage`: keeps your data in the browser.
- `scripting`: runs the extension on your VSA page only.
- Site access: requested for the single VSA address you enter, never for other sites.

## Data Deletion

- Delete rows, or use `Reset catalog`, in the extension.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.
